Noto Sans glyph ranges (SDF .pbf), from https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps-assets (fonts/).
Noto Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
At first launch the app copies these files to <documents>/glyphs/<font>/<range>.pbf,
which is where the map style's "glyphs" URL points (works offline).
