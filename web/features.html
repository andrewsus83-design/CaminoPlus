Features

Five tabs. One Camino.

Me, Stays, Maps, Discover and Library. Here is what each tab does, with screens from our app design.

MeStaysMapsDiscoverLibraryOffline

AI-generated illustration: pilgrims walking a street in Santiago

Me

Your Camino, your story.

A profile that feels like Instagram and a timeline like Facebook, built around the walk. Post your day, collect stamps in a digital credencial and keep track of every stage.

  • Self journey feed: tap the pen button, pick photos, write a caption, post
  • Passport & stamps: a digital credencial, each stamp with its story
  • Plan: a stage-by-stage tracker for where you sleep tonight and tomorrow
  • Health from your smartwatch: steps, distance and heart rate
  • Friends and messages behind the bell: friend requests, notifications, chats and group chats with pilgrims
  • Settings behind the gear at the top right: subscription and storage, account and password, light or dark mode, your smartwatch and your business listing
Stays

Albergues, hostels and cafés on your stage.

Search and filter places to sleep and eat along the way, then open a detail page with photos, beds, reviews and opening hours, the way you know from travel apps.

  • Filter by category, review score, distance, price and labels
  • Bookmark favourites for the days ahead
  • Detail pages with beds, amenities, reviews and popular times
  • Clear labels on what you can book in the app and what is walk-in only
Maps

Navigation that feels like an adventure.

A clear, Waze-style map with a touch of RPG. Zoom out to see the whole route from above, then pinch in to walk the streets of each town.

  • Two layers: helicopter view of the route and city view at street level
  • Churches, fountains and points of interest from OpenStreetMap. History pop-ups and audio stories Coming
  • Scavenger hunts, achievements and XP as you walk
  • Crowd updates from other pilgrims, and the weather ahead
  • Offline map for every stage you download with Pilgrim Plus: route, towns, water, churches and albergues
Discover

Stories from the trail, full screen.

Swipe through short, full-screen stories from pilgrims and places along the Way. Small tabs at the top switch topics, and a quick tutorial shows you how on your first visit.

  • Full-screen photo and video stories, swipe left and right
  • Topic tabs at the top for the kind of stories you want
  • Photos of you: buy shots other pilgrims took of you on the way Coming later
Library

Scripture, books and notes for the road.

A search field and your bookmarks along the top, then three tabs: Bible, Books and Notes. The Bible tab is real: all 73 books of the World English Bible, Catholic edition, a modern English translation in the public domain, are in the app, so you can read with no signal. It is not the translation read at Mass. A small banner for the daily reading comes first, then a pill to pick the part (Old Testament, Deuterocanon or New Testament) and the books of that part as square tiles. Tap a book, tap a chapter number and read; Previous and Next carry on across the books. The daily reading is an example today, and the app says so. The Books tab shows twelve classics for the road as covers, two in a row. A book marked Free is in the app: tap its cover and then Start reading, with a table of contents, Previous and Next, a text size you choose and your place kept on your phone only. A book marked Soon is not in the app yet while we check its edition, and its page says so. Notes is your private diary.

  • The whole Bible in the app: 73 books of the World English Bible, Catholic edition (public domain), readable with no signal
  • Pick a part, tap a book, tap a chapter number and read. Previous and Next name where they go and carry on from book to book, and Continue reading takes you back to the chapter you left (remembered only on your phone)
  • Tap a verse to bookmark it or share it. The search finds verses by their words and opens the chapter at that verse
  • The daily reading is an example for now Coming
  • The Books tab: twelve classics for the road, as covers. Books marked Free are in the app, so you can read them with no signal: Previous and Next name where they go, you choose the size of the text, and your place is kept only on your phone
  • Books marked Soon are not in the app yet while we check their editions Coming
  • Bookmarks also keep the posts you bookmark on your journey
  • Notes is a private diary: tap the gold button to write, and the page is saved on your phone as you type. Make words bold or italic, add headings and lists, and attach photos, videos and the place you are at
  • Nothing in the diary is uploaded or shared, so it is not backed up either. It works without a signal and stays on your phone when you log out

The screens are previews rendered from our app design. The app is still in development, so details can change before launch.

Under the hood

Made for real days on the trail.

Offline-first

With Pilgrim Plus, downloaded stages keep the map and your GPS position working for a full day without signal. Details of stays and audio stories need a connection for now.

Syncs when you are back

Posts and stamps go into an outbox and upload automatically when you reconnect.

Easy on battery

A location mode tuned for long walking days. Target: under 15% over 7 hours.

Crowd updates

Pilgrims share how busy the trail, albergues and cafés are, with an “updated X ago” label.

Weather ahead

See the forecast for where you will be this afternoon, not just where you are now.

Audio stories Coming

Listen to the history of churches, bridges and villages as you walk past.

Hunts & achievements

Scavenger hunts, XP and levels add a little play to every stage.

Apple Watch & Garmin

Steps, distance and heart rate on your profile, with your permission.

Private by design

You choose who sees each post. Export or delete your data any time.
