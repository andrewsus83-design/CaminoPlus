Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

Draft for launch. This text is a plain-language summary of how we intend to operate and will be reviewed by legal counsel before the app is released.

Who we are

CaminoPlus (“we”, “us”) makes the CaminoPlus app and this website. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at privacy@camino.plus.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, name and profile photo, or the basic profile Google shares if you sign in with Google.
  • Content you create: journal posts, photos, captions, comments, passport stamps and your stage plan.
  • Friends and messages: your friend list and friend requests (who asked whom, and when), the people you block, the messages you send in direct chats and group chats (including photos you attach), and the names and members of your groups.
  • Location: your device location while you use the map, to show where you are and to record your route. You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings.
  • Health and fitness data: only if you connect Apple Watch or Garmin, and only the data you allow, such as steps, distance and heart rate.
  • Purchase data: your subscription status from the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • Technical data: device type and app version, and crash reports once crash reporting is switched on, so we can fix problems.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, your journey, offline downloads and sync (contract).
  • To show your posts to the people you choose (contract, and your choices).
  • To deliver your messages and friend requests to the people you send them to (contract).
  • To process health data you connect (your explicit consent, which you can withdraw).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix bugs (legitimate interests).
  • To send service emails such as sign-in links and receipts, and launch news if you ask for it (contract or consent).

We do not sell personal data and we do not use third-party advertising trackers in the app.

Friends

Friends are mutual: you become friends when you send a request and the other person accepts. We store the request, who sent it and when. Declining or cancelling a request deletes it, and so does removing a friend. People you block cannot send you friend requests or messages, and you do not appear in their search. A post you share with “Friends” is shown to your accepted friends and to your followers. On a public profile anyone can follow you without asking; on a private profile you approve each follower.

Who sees what

  • Your journey (the posts you share, your profile card and its counts, and your route with its status, its planned start and end dates and its distance) is shown to the people each post or setting allows: only you, your friends and followers, or everyone.
  • Only for you: your plan day by day (the date of each day, where it starts and ends, stays, bookings and the notes of each day), your passport stamps (their notes, places and photos), your GPS tracks and your health data. Friends and followers do not see them.
  • Your notes in the app stay on your phone and are not uploaded.

Share links

You can share one plan or one note through a link. Anyone who has the link can see what it shows, until you stop sharing it in the app. Links can also be made to expire.

  • A plan link shows your plan as it is when the link is opened: the route, the start date, the distance, and for each day the date, where it starts and ends, its distance and whether it is walked. The name of the place you sleep is shown only if you choose. It never shows the notes of a day, bookings, contact details, health, GPS tracks or stamps.
  • A note link carries a copy of the words of that one note (its title, text and date), made when you share it and stored on our servers until you stop sharing it. Photos, videos and the place of the note are not included.
  • We store only a fingerprint (a hash) of each link, not the link itself, so we cannot give a lost link back; make a new one instead. We count how often a link is opened and when it was last opened, and show you both.
  • The link page on this website reads the link only in your browser and does not use cookies or analytics. When you delete your account, your links stop working and are deleted.

Chat messages and groups

  • Messages you send in direct chats and group chats are stored on our servers so they can reach the other people in the chat and appear on your other devices.
  • Only the people in a chat can read its messages in the app. Group members can also see each other's names and photos, and whether a message has been read.
  • Messages are protected in transit and at rest by our hosting provider, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. That means our team and our provider are technically able to read them. We only look at messages to investigate a report, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it. If someone reports a message, our team can read that message.
  • Please do not send passwords, card numbers or other secrets in chat.
  • You can unsend your own messages; they disappear for everyone in the chat. When you delete your account your messages are deleted too.
  • If you use the help bell, your position is sent only to the friends and groups you choose, as a chat message stored like any other message.

Who processes data for us

We use trusted service providers that act on our instructions under data processing agreements. In use today:

  • Supabase: accounts and sign-in, the database, and file storage (photos, chat images and the offline map downloads)
  • Vercel: hosting of this website
  • MapTiler and OpenFreeMap: the map tiles your phone loads while it is online. They receive your IP address and the area you are looking at. Offline maps come from our own storage, not from them.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution and payments, and Google sign-in if you choose it

Planned, and only used once switched on (we will update this page and its date first):

  • RevenueCat: subscription status, when enabled
  • Sentry: crash and error reports, when enabled
  • Resend: sign-in and service emails from our own domain, when enabled

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your data, including your messages, within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as billing records.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. That button is being finished and will be in the first release. Until it is in your version, email privacy@camino.plus from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account for you within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, write to privacy@camino.plus. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

Children

CaminoPlus is not intended for children under 16 without a parent or guardian's consent.

This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. Its fonts are served from our own server, not from Google Fonts. Our host, Vercel, processes technical request data such as your IP address to deliver the pages. The contact form only opens your email app with the message ready; nothing is sent until you send it from there. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, we will list them here.

Changes

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
