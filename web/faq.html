Getting started

What is CaminoPlus?

CaminoPlus is a mobile app for pilgrims walking the Camino de Santiago. It brings together offline maps, places to stay and eat, a digital pilgrim passport, a journal you can share, and stories from the trail, so you only need one app on the Way.

When can I download it?

The app is in development for iPhone and Android. The iOS and Android buttons on this site will turn into the store links at launch. Contact us or tap “Notify me at launch” and we will email you on the day.

Which phones are supported?

CaminoPlus is built for recent iPhones and Android phones. We will publish the exact minimum iOS and Android versions with the store listings.

What language is the app in?

English for now. More languages are planned.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free account lets you save your plan, passport and journal. You can sign up with email or with Google.

Offline & maps

Does CaminoPlus work without signal?

The map does. CaminoPlus is offline-first: with Pilgrim Plus you download your route stage by stage, and the map and your GPS position keep working for a full walking day without signal. The map shows roads, paths, towns, water, churches and albergues as they are mapped in OpenStreetMap. Details of stays, audio stories and elevation lines still need a connection for now; offline versions are coming. Anything you post while offline waits in a queue and syncs when you are back online.

How big are the offline downloads?

Each stage pack is roughly 2–5 MB. The first stage of the Camino Francés, Saint-Jean to Roncesvalles, is about 4.6 MB. You can download only the stages you need.

Will GPS drain my battery?

GPS works without mobile data, and CaminoPlus uses a battery-saving location mode built for long days on foot. Our target is under 15% battery over a 7-hour walk. Real numbers depend on your phone, screen use and cold weather, so carrying a small power bank is still wise.

Where does the map data come from?

Offline maps are built from OpenStreetMap, the free map made by a worldwide community. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. See Licences for everything the app is built on.

How fresh is information like crowds and opening hours?

Live information such as crowd updates is shown with a “updated X ago” label, so you always know how old it is. When you are offline you see the last version you downloaded.

Pricing & billing

How much does CaminoPlus cost?

The basic app is free. Pilgrim Plus costs $12 per year and adds offline route downloads and 5 GB of cloud storage. See Pricing for details.

What can I do on the free plan?

You can use the basic app with a connection: browse routes and stages, look up stays and cafés, watch stories in Discover and keep your passport and journal on your phone. The free plan has no offline route downloads and no cloud storage.

How do I pay?

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play, using the payment method on your store account. Prices may be shown in your local currency.

Does the subscription renew automatically?

Yes, yearly, until you cancel. You can turn off auto-renew at any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, and you keep access until the end of the paid year.

Can I get a refund?

Because payments go through Apple or Google, refunds follow their policies. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, request one from your Google Play order history. If something went wrong, tell us and we will help.

Cloud storage

What is the 5 GB of cloud storage for?

It backs up your journal posts, photos, passport stamps and plan, so nothing is lost if your phone is lost or broken, and you can open your journey on a new phone.

What happens if I need more space?

Add 5 GB more for $5 per year, as many times as you need.

What happens to my photos if my subscription ends?

Your posts stay on your phone. We will remind you before anything in cloud storage changes, and you can export your data at any time.

Smartwatch & health

Which smartwatches work with CaminoPlus?

Apple Watch and Garmin. Connect them in Settings.

What health data does it use?

With your permission, CaminoPlus reads walking data such as steps, distance and heart rate to show your daily progress on your profile. You choose what to share, and you can disconnect at any time.

Privacy & data

Is CaminoPlus GDPR compliant?

We design CaminoPlus around the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can see, export, correct and delete your data, and we only use processors that offer GDPR-ready data processing terms. Read the Privacy Policy.

Who can see my journey?

You choose who sees each post you share. Your journey is yours.

Are my messages private?

Messages in chats and group chats are only shown to the people in the chat, but they are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send passwords or card numbers in chat. You can unsend your own messages, and they are deleted with your account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell personal data, and we do not run third-party ad tracking in the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. That button is being finished and will be in the first release; until it is in your version, email privacy@camino.plus from the address you signed up with and we will do it for you. We delete your account and your data, including your messages, within 30 days, apart from what we must keep by law, such as billing records.

Routes

Which Camino routes are included?

All the main Camino de Santiago routes, including the Francés, Portugués (Central, Coastal and from Lisbon), del Norte, Primitivo, Vía de la Plata, Inglés, Finisterre and Muxía, and more. Stage plans and offline maps arrive route by route, starting with the Francés. See Routes for the full list.

Can I start anywhere?

Yes. Pick your route and the town where you start, and CaminoPlus builds your stages from there. You can change the plan as you go.

Does CaminoPlus give me a Compostela?

No. The Compostela is issued by the Pilgrim's Office in Santiago, based on the stamps in your paper credencial. To qualify you must walk at least the last 100 km (or cycle 200 km). The digital passport in CaminoPlus is a keepsake of your journey and does not replace the paper credencial.

Will other pilgrimage routes be added?

That is the plan. CaminoPlus is built so new routes can be added over time.

Community & safety

How do you keep the community friendly?

You can report and block users, posts and messages, and our team reviews reports. Our Terms explain what is not allowed.

Is the trail information always right?

We work hard to keep it accurate, but paths, opening hours and conditions change. Always check signs on the ground, follow local advice and use your own judgement, especially in bad weather.
