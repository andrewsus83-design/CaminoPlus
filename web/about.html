About

Made for pilgrims, by people who love the Way.

CaminoPlus started from a simple wish: one app that works on the trail, keeps the memories, and helps you find a bed at the end of the day.

AI-generated illustration: a scallop waymarker with a yellow arrow

Walking to Santiago today often means juggling a guidebook, a map app that needs signal, a booking site, a notes app and a camera roll full of photos you never sort. Each one does part of the job. None of them were made for the Camino.

CaminoPlus brings it together. The map works when the signal doesn't. Stays and cafés are on the same screen as your route. Your stamps live in a digital passport next to the story of each place. And at the end of the day, you can post your journey the way you already know how.

We start with the Camino de Santiago, every route, starting wherever you choose. Later we want to bring CaminoPlus to other pilgrim routes around the world.

What we believe

Our principles.

Offline first

The Camino is not a city. We design for the hill with no signal, not the café with Wi-Fi.

Respect the Way

The Camino is a pilgrimage before it is a trip. The app should help you walk, then get out of the way.

Open map data

Our maps are built on OpenStreetMap, made by volunteers, and we credit them everywhere.

Your journey is yours

You choose who sees what. We do not sell data and you can take your data with you.

Run an albergue or café?

We want every place on the Way to be easy to find. Get in touch to check or update your listing.
