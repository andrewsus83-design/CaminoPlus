Questions

Good to know before you go.

Does CaminoPlus work without signal?

The map does, if you have Pilgrim Plus. Offline maps are only available to paying members. With Pilgrim Plus you download your route stage by stage, and the map and your GPS position keep working for a full walking day without signal. The map shows roads, paths, towns, water, churches and albergues as they are mapped in OpenStreetMap. Details of stays, audio stories and elevation lines still need a connection for now; offline versions are coming. Anything you post while offline waits in a queue and syncs when you are back online.

How much does CaminoPlus cost?

The basic app is free. Pilgrim Plus costs $12 per year and adds offline route downloads and 5 GB of cloud storage. See Pricing for details.

How big are the offline downloads?

A typical stage pack is about 4 MB, and most are between 1 and 6 MB. Stages through big cities such as Pamplona and Burgos are larger, up to about 11 MB. The first stage of the Camino Francés, Saint-Jean to Roncesvalles, is about 4.6 MB. You can download only the stages you need.

Can I start anywhere?

Yes. Pick your route and the town where you start, and CaminoPlus builds your stages from there. You can change the plan as you go.
