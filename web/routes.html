Routes

Every Camino, from wherever you start.

CaminoPlus covers the Camino de Santiago routes across Spain, Portugal and France. Choose a route, choose your starting town, and the app builds your stages.

Camino Francés

Most walked

From Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, France

The classic. Across the Pyrenees, the Meseta and into Galicia.

~780 km31–35 stages

Camino Portugués (Central)

Popular

From Porto (or Lisbon), Portugal

Through northern Portugal's villages and vineyards. About 620 km and 25–29 stages if you start in Lisbon.

~240 km10–13 stages

Camino Portugués (Coastal)

From Porto, Portugal

Atlantic beaches and fishing towns, joining the Central route at Redondela.

~260 km11–13 stages

Camino del Norte

From Irún, Spain

Along the Bay of Biscay through San Sebastián, Bilbao and Cantabria.

~825 km33–38 stages

Camino Primitivo

From Oviedo, Spain

The original route. Mountains, quiet paths and big climbs.

~310 km12–15 stages

Vía de la Plata

From Seville, Spain

The longest in Spain, following an old Roman road north.

~1,000 km38–42 stages

Camino Inglés

From Ferrol or A Coruña, Spain

Short and green, the route of pilgrims who arrived by sea.

~120 km5–6 stages

Camino Finisterre & Muxía

From Santiago de Compostela

Keep walking past Santiago to the coast at the 'end of the world'.

~120 km3–5 stages

Camino Sanabrés

From Granja de Moreruela, Spain

The Vía de la Plata's branch through Ourense and its hot springs.

~370 km15–17 stages

Camino de Invierno

From Ponferrada, Spain

The winter route through the Sil valley and Ribeira Sacra.

~265 km10–12 stages

Camino de Madrid

From Madrid, Spain

From the capital over the Guadarrama to join the Francés at Sahagún.

~320 km12–14 stages

Camino Aragonés

From Somport, Spain

Down from the Pyrenees to meet the Francés at Puente la Reina.

~160 km6–7 stages

Via Podiensis (Le Puy)

From Le Puy-en-Velay, France

France's best-known route, ending at Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port.

~740 km28–32 stages

Distances and stage counts are approximate and depend on where you start and how far you walk each day.

Start anywhere

Begin in Saint-Jean, Sarria, Porto or any town on the route. Your stages, distances and stays adjust to you.

Download stage by stage

Only take what you need. Each offline stage pack is about 2–5 MB.

The last 100 km

For a Compostela you must walk at least the final 100 km, so plan your start town with that in mind.
