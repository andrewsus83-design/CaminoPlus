Offline maps

Signal drops. Your map doesn't.

Large parts of the Camino have weak or no mobile signal. CaminoPlus is built offline-first, so the map and your position keep working with your phone in airplane mode.

AI-generated illustration: a pilgrim and a waymarker above the clouds

How it works

Four steps, then just walk.

Pick your route

Choose a Camino and the town you start from. CaminoPlus lists your stages.

Download over Wi-Fi

At home or at the albergue, download the next stages. A typical pack is about 4 MB.

Walk without signal

The map and your GPS position work offline: roads, paths, towns, water, churches and albergues.

Sync when you're back

Posts and stamps you made offline upload on their own when you reconnect.

Small downloads

A whole stage in a few megabytes.

Stage packs are compact vector maps, not heavy images, so they download quickly even on albergue Wi-Fi and barely touch your phone storage. Stage 1 of the Camino Francés, Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port to Roncesvalles over the Pyrenees, is about 4.6 MB. Measured over the whole Camino Francés, a typical stage is about 4 MB and most are between 1 and 6 MB; stages through big cities such as Pamplona and Burgos are larger, up to about 11 MB. More stages are being added.

Inside each pack

What works offline.

Vector map

Roads, paths, buildings, towns, villages and water, crisp at every zoom level, with place names that are drawn offline too.

Stage by stage

Each pack covers one stage and a few kilometres either side of the route, so you only download what you walk.

Churches, albergues and water

Churches, albergues and drinking-water points, as they are mapped in OpenStreetMap.

Stays, stories, elevation Coming

Details of stays, audio stories and elevation lines are not in the packs yet. They need a connection for now; we will add them.

Battery-saving GPS

GPS needs no mobile data. CaminoPlus uses a location mode tuned for walking, with a target of under 15% battery over a 7-hour day.

Outbox that syncs itself

Anything you post, stamp or plan offline is saved on your phone and uploaded in order when you have signal again.

“Updated X ago”

Live info like crowds always shows when it was last refreshed, so you know how fresh it is.

Built on OpenStreetMap

Offline maps are made from OpenStreetMap, the free, community-built map of the world, which is especially detailed along the Camino. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. See Licences for the full list.

Offline downloads are part of Pilgrim Plus ($12 a year).
