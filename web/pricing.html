Billing questions

Pricing FAQ

How much does CaminoPlus cost?

The basic app is free. Pilgrim Plus costs $11.99 per year and adds offline route downloads and 5 GB of cloud storage. See Pricing for details.

What can I do on the free plan?

You can use the basic app with a connection: browse routes and stages, look up stays and cafés, watch stories in Explore and keep your passport and journal on your phone. The free plan has no offline route downloads and no cloud storage.

How do I pay?

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play, using the payment method on your store account. Prices may be shown in your local currency.

Does the subscription renew automatically?

Yes, yearly, until you cancel. You can turn off auto-renew at any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, and you keep access until the end of the paid year.

Can I get a refund?

Because payments go through Apple or Google, refunds follow their policies. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, request one from your Google Play order history. If something went wrong, tell us and we will help.

What is the 5 GB of cloud storage for?

It backs up your journal posts, photos, passport stamps and plan, so nothing is lost if your phone is lost or broken, and you can open your journey on a new phone.

What happens if I need more space?

Add 5 GB more for $5 per year, as many times as you need.

What happens to my photos if my subscription ends?

Your posts stay on your phone. We will remind you before anything in cloud storage changes, and you can export your data at any time.
