# CaminoPlus Brand Guideline

Version 1.1 · November 2026 · Scope: social media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube Shorts, Threads, X, WhatsApp Channel), also valid for ads, app store screenshots and press.

The source of truth for colours, type and spacing in code is [`design/tokens.json`](../design/tokens.json). This document explains how to use them in content.

---

## 1. Brand in one page

**What we are.** CaminoPlus is the offline-first companion app for the Camino de Santiago. Maps, stays, a digital pilgrim passport and a shareable journal, for every route, from wherever you start. It works when the signal doesn't.

**Promise.** *Every step of the Way, even offline.*

**Stance.** CaminoPlus is a Christian-rooted companion for the Way, and every walker is welcome.

**Who we talk to.**

| Persona | Who | What they need from us |
|---|---|---|
| The Dreamer | 25 to 60, thinking about walking "one day", saving Camino posts | Inspiration, reassurance it's doable, a first step |
| The Planner | Booked flights, 1 to 6 months out | Route choice, stages, packing, budget, where to sleep |
| The Walker | On the Camino right now | Offline map, next café, albergue beds, weather, stamps |
| The Returner | Walked before, nostalgic, often walks again | Memories, community, new routes to try |

**Personality.** Warm, calm, capable, a little playful. A seasoned friend who has walked it, not a tour operator and not a guru.

**We are / we are not.**

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Calm and reassuring | Hype, FOMO, "must-see before you die" |
| Practical, specific (km, stages, opening times) | Vague inspiration with nothing useful |
| Rooted in the Christian pilgrimage and open to every reason to walk (faith, sport, grief, curiosity) | Preachy, pressuring, or shy about our Christian roots |
| Respectful of pilgrims, hosts and locals | Mocking, or treating villages as backdrops |
| Honest (approximate figures marked with "~") | Overclaiming ("the #1 Camino app") |

## 2. Voice and tone

**Voice rules**

1. **Talk like a fellow pilgrim.** Second person, short sentences, plain English. "Your feet will thank you" beats "Optimise your trekking experience".
2. **Lead with something useful.** Every post gives a fact, a tip, a feeling, or a decision. Ideally two.
3. **Be specific.** Name the place, the km, the stage. "Sarria to Santiago is ~115 km, the most popular start for the last 100 km of the Compostela" beats "the last stretch".
4. **Warm, never cheesy.** One emoji per caption is fine, three is the limit. Allowed favourites: 🐚 🥾 ☀️ 🗺️ ☕ 🌧️.
5. **Spanish/Portuguese words are welcome** when pilgrims really use them: *Buen Camino*, *albergue*, *credencial*, *café con leche*, *hospitalero*, *sello*. Explain the first time if needed.
6. **"The Way" and "the Camino"** are capitalised. "Pilgrim", not "user" or "customer".

**Tone by situation**

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Inspiration | Quiet, poetic, short | "The Meseta isn't empty. It's room to think." |
| Tips / planning | Clear, list-like, confident | "3 things to check before Stage 1: credencial, cash, offline map." |
| Product | Benefit first, feature second | "No signal in the Galician forest? Your map is already on your phone." |
| Community reply | Friendly, quick, personal | "Buen Camino, Marta! Which stage are you on?" |
| Safety / weather | Calm, direct, no jokes | "Heat warning on the Meseta today. Start before 7, carry 2 L of water." |
| Problem / complaint | Own it, move to DM | "Sorry, that's not okay. Sending you a DM to fix it now." |
| Scripture and faith | Quiet, short, sourced | One verse at most, always with its book, chapter and verse. Use a paraphrase or the app's own translation, never one we have not cleared. |

**Words we use / avoid**

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| walk, pilgrimage, the Way, stage, albergue, stay | trip package, tour, hack, crush it |
| offline, no signal needed, works on the trail | revolutionary, game-changing, AI-powered (unless it really is) |
| ~ (approximate) for distances | exact claims we haven't verified |
| "from $12 a year" | "cheap", "free" (only the free tier is free) |
| a verse with its reference, a quiet line | "find God", "be saved", "miracle" as hype, "real pilgrim" |

**Taglines and lines we can reuse**

- Every step of the Way, *even offline.* (primary)
- Signal drops. Your map doesn't.
- Five tabs. One Camino.
- Less than a café con leche a month.
- Every Camino, from wherever you start.
- Buen Camino. 🐚 (sign-off)

## 3. Logo

The logo is **locked** (PR #9). Navy circle mark with a gold 7-rib scallop shell, then "Camino" in Fraunces Bold and a bold gold "+" (Inter Bold). The earlier shell-as-"o" wordmark was retired. Master files: [`web/favicon.svg`](../web/favicon.svg), [`web/assets/apple-touch-icon.png`](../web/assets/apple-touch-icon.png), [`web/assets/og.png`](../web/assets/og.png).

**Versions**

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Full wordmark, Atlantic Blue on light | Default on parchment or light photos |
| Full wordmark, Parchment on dark | On Atlantic Blue or dark photos |
| Shell icon in circle (no text) | Profile pictures, watermark, stickers, app icon |

**Rules**

- Profile picture on all platforms: shell icon, Shell Gold circle on Atlantic Blue (or reverse), centred, no text.
- Clear space around the logo = the height of the capital "C" in "Camino" on every side (about half the height of the shell mark).
- Minimum size: wordmark 96 px wide, icon 32 px.
- Watermark on posts: small icon or wordmark, bottom right or bottom centre, 70 to 100% opacity, inside the safe zone (see section 7).
- Do not: recolour outside the palette, stretch, rotate, add shadows/outlines, rebuild the shell, separate the "+" and move it, put it on a busy part of a photo.

## 4. Colour

| Name | Hex | Role on social |
|---|---|---|
| Atlantic Blue | `#14365D` | Main text, dark backgrounds, logo |
| Shell Gold | `#D9A21B` | Highlights, numbers, stickers, CTA backgrounds. **Never for body text on light** |
| Gold Ink | `#7A5A0C` | Gold-coloured text on light backgrounds |
| Parchment | `#F6EFE2` | Main light background, text on blue |
| Cross Red | `#A63D2F` | Rare accent: warnings, "new", a single word. Max ~5% of a design |
| Gold Wash | `#FBF3DC` | Soft card background |
| Sand 300 | `#E4D7BF` | Dividers, quiet frames |
| Stone 600 | `#556274` | Secondary text |

**Proportion per design (rule of thumb):** 60% Parchment or photo, 25% Atlantic Blue, 10% Shell Gold, 5% Cross Red or nothing.

**Approved pairs (readable):** Blue on Parchment, Parchment on Blue, Blue on Gold, Gold Ink on Gold Wash, Gold on Blue (headlines only, 28 px+).
**Do not pair:** Gold text on Parchment/white, Red on Blue, Red on Gold.

## 5. Typography

| Font | Role | Social use |
|---|---|---|
| **Fraunces** SemiBold 600 | Titles | Hooks, headlines, big numbers. Sentence case. Tight letter spacing |
| **Inter** Regular/SemiBold | Body | Carousel body text, lists, subtitles, captions on video |
| **JetBrains Mono** | Data | km, elevation, stage numbers, coordinates, "Stage 07 · 24.6 km" |
| **Caveat** Bold | Handwritten accent | One short word or note: "you've got this", "Day 12", arrows. Max one per design |

All four are free on Google Fonts. In Canva use the same names (all available).

**Sizes for a 1080 px wide canvas**

| Element | Size |
|---|---|
| Hook / headline | 72 to 110 px Fraunces |
| Sub-headline | 40 to 56 px Fraunces |
| Body | 32 to 40 px Inter (never below 28 px) |
| Data label | 28 to 34 px JetBrains Mono |
| Accent note | 48 to 72 px Caveat |

Max ~25 words of on-image text per frame. If it needs more, it belongs in the caption.

## 6. Photography and imagery

The look is **natural documentary travel photography**: true-to-life colour, soft light, real textures, lots of breathing space. It is the same look as the website; the prompt builder for AI images is in [`content-factory/templates.md`](content-factory/templates.md#6-ai-image-prompt-builder).

**Do**

- Golden hour, morning mist, soft overcast. Warm, calm colours that sit well with blue and gold.
- Wide landscapes with a tiny pilgrim silhouette, path details, yellow arrows, scallop shell markers, stamps in a credencial, boots, café tables, albergue bunks, rain gear.
- Leave empty sky or empty side space for text.
- Real phone-in-hand shots of the app on the trail (screen readable, no glare).

**Don't**

- Heavy filters, HDR, teal-and-orange grading, oversaturated skies.
- Stock-looking posed smiles, drone shots that look like ads.
- Clear faces of strangers without permission. Children never without parental consent.
- Fake or "improved" landmarks.

**AI-generated images.** Allowed for mood and landscapes. Rules: (1) never pass off a real landmark that AI got wrong as real; check towers, doors and bridges against real photos, and prefer real photos for Santiago Cathedral and other famous buildings; (2) label "AI illustration" in the caption or alt text when the place is real; (3) no AI faces presented as real pilgrims; (4) no fake app reviews or testimonials, ever.

**User-generated content (UGC).** Always ask permission in writing (DM or comment "yes"), credit with @handle in the first line, keep their original colours (light crop only).

**App screens.** Use the screens from [`web/assets/screens/`](../web/assets/screens/) (rendered from the app design, `tools/render_screens.mjs`) inside a simple phone frame. The app is still in development, so call them previews. Don't mock features that don't exist yet; if a feature is "coming soon", say so. The fourth tab is called **Discover**.

## 7. Layout for social

**Formats and safe zones**

| Format | Size | Safe zone (keep text and logo inside) |
|---|---|---|
| Feed portrait (IG, FB) | 1080 × 1350 | 60 px margin all sides |
| Carousel slide | 1080 × 1350 | 60 px margin; keep slide number top right |
| Reels / TikTok / Stories | 1080 × 1920 | Top 250 px and bottom 400 px empty; right 140 px empty (buttons) |
| Square (FB, ads, WhatsApp) | 1080 × 1080 | 60 px margin |
| Pinterest pin | 1000 × 1500 | 60 px margin; headline in top half; logo bottom centre |
| YouTube Shorts | 1080 × 1920 | Same as Reels; bottom 450 px empty (title + buttons) |
| X / Threads image | 1200 × 675 or 1080 × 1350 | 60 px margin |
| Cover / grid crop | Centre 1080 × 1080 of a 1350 post | Hook must read in the square crop |

**Layout rules**

- Spacious. One idea per frame. If in doubt, remove something.
- Pill shapes (fully rounded) for tags, CTAs and stickers: "Stage 07", "Offline ✓", "Save this".
- Cards: 28 to 36 px corner radius, Parchment or White `#FFFDF8`, soft blue shadow.
- Text on photos: place it on the empty sky/side; if needed, a soft gradient from Atlantic Blue at 0 to 60%, never a black box.
- Data chips use JetBrains Mono inside a pill: `24.6 km · ↑ 520 m · 6 h`.
- Logo on every designed post (not needed on UGC reposts, where we credit instead).

**Recurring visual systems** (so the grid looks like one brand):

| Series | Look |
|---|---|
| Stage Cards | Photo top 60%, Parchment panel bottom with Fraunces stage name + Mono data chips |
| Tip carousels | Parchment background, big Fraunces number in Shell Gold on Blue circle, Inter body |
| Quotes / reflections | Full-bleed photo, one Fraunces line, Caveat signature |
| Product | Atlantic Blue background, phone frame, gold pill CTA |
| Pilgrim stories | UGC photo, Parchment strip with name + route + day in Mono |

## 8. Motion and video (Reels, TikTok)

- Hook in the first 1.5 seconds: big Fraunces text or a strong visual (arrow, shell, cliff, rain).
- Length: 7 to 25 s for reach, 30 to 60 s for tutorials.
- Cuts on the beat, but gentle. No shaky zooms, no glitch effects.
- On-screen captions always (most people watch muted): Inter SemiBold, white or Parchment with a soft blue shadow, inside the safe zone.
- Sound: ambient trail sound (steps, birds, church bells, rain) or calm acoustic/trending sound at low volume. Check music rights for business accounts (use the platforms' commercial libraries).
- End card (1 s): shell icon + "Buen Camino" or the CTA.

## 9. Community and comments

- Reply to every comment and DM within 24 h in the first months.
- Use the pilgrim's name. End with a question when natural.
- Faith-related comments: warm, never debating. We are Christian-rooted and we serve every pilgrim. Offer a prayer only if someone asks.
- Safety questions (heat, injury, lost): give clear basics and point to official help (112 in Spain and Portugal). Never give medical advice.
- Negative reviews: acknowledge, apologise if we're wrong, move to DM. Never argue, never delete honest criticism (delete only spam, hate, or personal data).
- Don't promise launch dates or features that aren't confirmed.

## 10. Legal and honesty basics

- Distances and stage counts are approximate; write "~" as on the website.
- Pricing: "$12 a year" for Pilgrim Plus; free tier exists. Don't invent discounts.
- Partner or paid posts must be marked (#ad / "Paid partnership" label).
- Respect location rules: no drone footage in restricted areas, no filming inside churches during services.
- Credit photographers and UGC; keep the permission screenshot in the content folder.
