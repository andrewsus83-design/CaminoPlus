# Platform playbook

Where CaminoPlus shares content, what each platform is for, and how to repurpose one piece across all of them.

| Tier | Platform | Why it matters for pilgrims | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | **Instagram** | Main visual home: Reels, carousels, Stories, Highlights | High |
| Core | **TikTok** | Biggest reach for new Dreamers | High |
| Core | **Facebook** | Older pilgrims (40+), very active Camino groups | Medium |
| Growth | **Pinterest** | People plan trips here for months; pins keep sending traffic for years (search engine, not a feed) | Low (reuse) |
| Growth | **YouTube Shorts** (+ long YouTube later) | Search for "Camino de Santiago" is huge on YouTube; Shorts reuse Reels | Low (reuse) |
| Growth | **Threads** | Conversation, quick tips, founder voice; links to Instagram | Low |
| Optional | **X (Twitter)** | Launch news, press, tech/founder audience | Low |
| Optional | **WhatsApp Channel** | Direct, high open rate; "Camino tip of the week" and launch alert | Low |
| Later | **YouTube long-form** | Route guides 8 to 15 min, when there is footage and time | High |
| Later | **LinkedIn** | Partners, investors, albergue/café commission partners | Low |

Start with Core + Growth. Add Optional once the core rhythm runs for 4 weeks without missing posts.

## Per platform

### Pinterest
- **Format:** vertical pins 1000 × 1500 (2:3), also Idea/video pins 1080 × 1920.
- **Content:** Stage Cards, Tip Carousel slide 1 as a pin, packing lists, route maps, "Camino de Santiago itinerary 7 days" type infographics.
- **Text on pin:** keyword headline in Fraunces, e.g. "Camino Portugués from Porto: ~240 km guide". Logo bottom centre.
- **Description:** keyword-rich, 2 to 3 sentences, no hashtag stuffing (0 to 3).
- **Link:** every pin links to a page on camino.plus (routes, offline maps, pricing, or a future blog post).
- **Boards:** Camino Francés · Camino Portugués · Camino del Norte & Primitivo · Packing & Gear · Planning & Budget · Camino Inspiration · Santiago de Compostela.
- **Cadence:** 5 to 10 pins a week (mostly re-sized existing posts), scheduled with Pinterest's own scheduler.

### YouTube Shorts
- **Format:** same 9:16 Reels/TikTok file, ≤60 s, no TikTok watermark.
- **Title:** searchable, ≤60 chars, e.g. "Camino Francés in 30 seconds #camino".
- **Description:** 1 line + link to camino.plus + 3 hashtags.
- **Cadence:** every Reel goes here too (3 to 4 a week).
- **Later:** long-form guides ("Camino Portugués: everything you need in 12 minutes") once footage exists.

### Threads
- **Format:** text first, 1 to 4 short lines, optional 1 image or carousel.
- **Content:** tip of the day, questions ("Francés or Portugués for a first Camino?"), building-in-public notes from the founder, replies to Camino conversations.
- **Tone:** most casual of all channels, still on-voice.
- **Cadence:** 3 to 5 a week; cross-post selected carousels.

### X (Twitter)
- **Format:** short post + image or video, threads for guides.
- **Content:** launch updates, press mentions, product changes, one thread per hero topic.
- **Cadence:** 2 to 3 a week. Don't chase trends.

### WhatsApp Channel
- **Format:** one message, one image, one link.
- **Content:** "Camino tip of the week" (Monday), launch alert, big news. Never more than 2 a week.
- **CTA:** join link in bio and on the website footer.

### Facebook groups (community, not a channel we own)
- Large Camino groups exist and have strict self-promo rules. **Read each group's rules first.**
- Join as a person (founder), answer questions helpfully, share our posts only when they answer a question and the group allows it. Never spam links.

## Handles to claim

Claim **@caminoplus** on every platform above now (even the "later" ones) so nobody else takes it. Fallbacks: @camino.plus, @caminoplusapp. Use the same profile picture and bio everywhere (see [Brand Guideline §11](../BRAND_GUIDELINE.md#11-handles-and-bios-to-set-up)).

## Making posts people share

Sharing is the cheapest growth. Build these into every week:

- **"Send this to your Camino buddy"** as the CTA on at least 2 posts a week.
- **Tag-a-friend** posts (Who would you walk with?), debate posts (boots or trail runners), and polls.
- **Save-worthy resources**: checklists, stage cards, budget tables. Saves and shares travel together.
- **Shareable Stories**: templates with an empty space ("My Camino starts in ___") that followers post and tag @caminoplus.
- **In the app later**: share journal posts, passport stamps and "Day X" cards with the Camino+ logo; every share is an ad.
- **Website**: add share buttons and the social links in the footer once the handles are live.
