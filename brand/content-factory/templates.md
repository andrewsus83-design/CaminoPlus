## 2. Hook bank

Use the first line of a caption, the first frame of a video and slide 1 of a carousel.

- `[Number] things I wish I knew before walking the [route].`
- `No signal on [place]? Here's what to do.`
- `[Route] in [N] seconds.`
- `Stage [N]: [start] to [end]. Is it as hard as they say?`
- `The cheapest way to walk the Camino in [year].`
- `Save this before your first stage.`
- `Francés or Portugués? Here's how to choose.`
- `Nobody tells you this about the Meseta.`
- `Why is the scallop shell the symbol of the Camino?`
- `What [N] km on foot actually feels like.`
- `Pilgrims, settle this: [question]?`
- `POV: it's 6 a.m. in [village] and the café isn't open yet.`

## 4. Reel / TikTok scripts

**Route Explainer (F03), 25–35 s**
```
0–2 s   Text: "[Route] in 30 seconds"  · Visual: map line drawing from start to Santiago
2–8 s   VO/text: "~[km] km, [N] stages, from [start]." · chips: km · days
8–18 s  3 highlights, 3 s each (photo + one word label)
18–25 s "Best for: [who]. Hardest part: [x]."
25–30 s End card: "Save this · Buen Camino 🐚"
```

**App Demo (F06), 15–25 s**
```
0–2 s   Text: "No signal. Watch this." · Visual: phone with 0 bars in the forest
2–6 s   Open CaminoPlus → map loads offline (screen recording)
6–14 s  Show 2 actions: next café / albergue, your blue dot on the path
14–20 s Text: "Download your stages once. Walk all day offline."
20–25 s End card: logo + "Coming soon · link in bio"
```

**Trail Moment (F04), 7–12 s**
```
One continuous or 2–3 cut ambient shot, natural sound.
One on-screen line (Fraunces, ≤8 words), appears at 1 s.
Small logo bottom centre in the last second.
```

**Myth or Fact (F10), 15–25 s**
```
0–2 s   Text: "Myth or fact? [claim]"
2–5 s   Pause beat with "🤔" sticker (drives comments)
5–18 s  Answer + 2 facts (source in caption)
18–22 s "Follow for more Camino stories 🐚"
```

## 6. AI image prompt builder

`[SCENE] + [LIGHT/TIME] + [COMPOSITION] + [STYLE SUFFIX] + [FORMAT]`

**Style suffix (always):**
```
natural documentary travel photograph, shot on a full-frame mirrorless camera, 35mm lens, f/8, true-to-life colors, soft natural light, subtle film grain, realistic textures, no people in the foreground, no text, no watermark, no logo, no signage
```

**Composition add-ons (pick one):**
- Feed with headline: `empty sky in the top third for text`
- Stage Card: `subject in the top half, calm simple foreground at the bottom`
- Story / Reel cover: `vertical composition, main subject in the centre third, empty space top and bottom`

**Format:**
- Midjourney: `--ar 4:5` (feed), `--ar 9:16` (Story/Reel), `--style raw --s 150 --v 7`
- ChatGPT / DALL·E: start with "Portrait 4:5 photo" or "Vertical 9:16 photo"

**Scene bank (add to it):**
- `a yellow arrow painted on a mossy stone wall beside a dirt path in rural Galicia, morning mist`
- `a worn pilgrim credencial with colourful ink stamps on a wooden café table next to a café con leche`
- `muddy hiking boots and a backpack with a scallop shell resting outside a stone albergue door`
- `endless green wheat fields of the Spanish Meseta in spring, a straight dirt path to the horizon`
- `rain on a narrow path through Galician eucalyptus forest, a hiker in a poncho far away`
- `sunrise over red-roofed village and vineyards in La Rioja, autumn colours`
- `the Atlantic coast at sunset with a wooden boardwalk in northern Portugal`
- `a stone waymarker with a carved scallop shell, shallow depth of field`

Remember the AI rules in the [Brand Guideline](../BRAND_GUIDELINE.md#6-photography-and-imagery): check real landmarks, label "AI illustration" for real places, no fake people as testimonials.

