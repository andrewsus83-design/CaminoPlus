# Pillars, formats, cadence, seasonality

## Content pillars

| # | Pillar | Share | Goal | Persona | Topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Inspire** (The Way) | 30% | Reach, follows | Dreamer, Returner | Landscapes, routes, small moments, reflections, "why people walk" |
| P2 | **Guide** (Plan & Walk) | 30% | Saves, trust | Planner, Walker | Route choice, stages, packing, budget, albergues, credencial, weather, safety |
| P3 | **Product** (CaminoPlus) | 20% | Sign-ups, downloads | Planner, Walker | Offline maps, stays, passport & stamps, journal, Maps RPG, pricing |
| P4 | **Community** (Pilgrims) | 15% | Engagement, UGC | All | Pilgrim stories, Q&A, polls, reposts, "where are you walking from?" |
| P5 | **Culture** (Stories of the Way) | 5% | Depth, shares | Dreamer, Returner | History, legends, churches, food, local words, the scallop shell |

Every post has exactly one main pillar.

## The 10 repeatable formats

| Code | Format | Platform | Pillar | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | **Stage Card** | IG feed, FB | Guide | One stage: photo + name + `km · ↑m · h` + 3 highlights + 1 tip |
| F02 | **Tip Carousel** | IG, FB (as album) | Guide | "5 things…" 6 to 8 slides, save CTA |
| F03 | **Route Explainer** | Reel, TikTok | Guide | 20 to 40 s: route in a map animation, km, best for whom |
| F04 | **Trail Moment** | Reel, TikTok | Inspire | 7 to 12 s ambient clip + one line of text |
| F05 | **Reflection Quote** | IG feed, FB | Inspire | Full-bleed photo + one Fraunces line |
| F06 | **App Demo** | Reel, TikTok | Product | "No signal? Watch this." 15 to 25 s screen + trail footage |
| F07 | **Feature Carousel** | IG, FB | Product | Problem → feature → how it works → CTA |
| F08 | **Pilgrim Story** | IG carousel, FB | Community | UGC photos + short interview, credited |
| F09 | **Ask the Camino** | Stories, TikTok | Community | Poll / quiz / question box; answers become posts |
| F10 | **Myth or Fact / Did you know** | Reel, TikTok, carousel | Culture | Legend or history explained in 20 s or 5 slides |

## Weekly cadence

Baseline for a small team (≈ 9 feed pieces + daily Stories per week). Each video goes to all three video placements.

| Day | Instagram | TikTok | Facebook | Stories (IG + FB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | F02 Tip Carousel (Guide) | F03 Route Explainer | Same carousel as album | Poll from the carousel topic |
| Tue | — | F04 Trail Moment | — | Behind the scenes / question box |
| Wed | F01 Stage Card (Guide) | F10 Myth or Fact | Stage Card | Quiz |
| Thu | F06 App Demo Reel (Product) | F06 App Demo | Reel | Link sticker "Notify me" |
| Fri | F05 Reflection or F08 Pilgrim Story | F04 Trail Moment | Same as IG | Repost UGC |
| Sat | F04 Trail Moment Reel (Inspire) | F09 Ask the Camino (reply video) | Reel | "Where are you walking from?" |
| Sun | — (rest, or community reposts) | — | Weekly question post | Weekly recap |

**Extra platforms each week** (mostly reused, see [`platforms.md`](platforms.md)):

**Lite version** (if time is short): Mon F02, Wed F01, Thu F06, Sat F04 + 3 Stories a week.

## Seasonality

The Camino has a strong yearly rhythm. Plan the hero topic per month with it.

| Month | Camino moment | Hero topic ideas |
|---|---|---|
| Jan | Dreaming and deciding | "Is this your year?", choosing a route |
| Feb | Planning, flights | Budget, how many days, credencial |
| Mar | Gear | Packing list, boots, breaking in |
| Apr | Season opens; Holy Week (Semana Santa) | First pilgrims, spring weather, Easter traditions |
| May | Peak spring | Flowers, Meseta green, crowd tips |
| Jun | Busy, long days | Heat, early starts, Portugués coastal |
| Jul | Peak; **25 July Feast of St James** | Santiago celebrations, botafumeiro, booking beds |
| Aug | Peak, hot | Heat safety, Norte and Primitivo (cooler), crowds |
| Sep | Second peak, harvest | Vineyards (La Rioja), best month debates |
| Oct | Autumn colour | Galicia forests, rain gear |
| Nov | Quiet | Winter Camino, which albergues stay open, reflection |
| Dec | Off-season, gifts | Gift for a pilgrim, year in review, planning next year |

Also: Holy Years (Año Santo Jacobeo) happen when 25 July falls on a Sunday; the next one is 2027. That is a big hook for the whole of 2027: start the "Holy Year 2027" series from early 2027.
