# Other ways to split the road

## Three ways to count

The sources keep to the old stage towns, but they do not agree on how many days there are, or on how long each is. This book uses the first split below, the one Visit Navarra proposes. The French association of the Pyrénées-Atlantiques and a pilgrim-hostel directory propose the others.

- **Visit Navarra's six stages, Bayonne to Pamplona, ~109 km.** Bayonne to Ustaritz ~14 km. Ustaritz to Urdazubi/Urdax ~21 km. Urdazubi/Urdax to Elizondo ~19 km. Elizondo to Berroeta ~10 km. Berroeta to Olagüe ~20 km. Olagüe to Pamplona ~25 km. Eroski Consumer gives the same figures.
- **The French association's five stages, ~108 km.** Bayonne to Souraïde ~25 km. Souraïde to Amaiur ~21 km. Amaiur to Berroeta ~19 km. Berroeta to Olagüe ~20 km. Olagüe to Pamplona ~24 km. This plan passes through Urdax and Elizondo without stopping.
- **The directory's six stages, Bayonne to Arre, ~103 km.** Bayonne to Ustaritz ~14 km. Ustaritz to Urdazubi/Urdax ~22 km. Urdazubi/Urdax to Amaiur/Maya ~10 km. Amaiur/Maya to Berroeta ~18 km. Berroeta to Olagüe ~20 km. Olagüe to Arre ~20 km. This is the only plan that ends at the junction with the French Way, not in Pamplona, and it puts the climb to Otsondo on a short day.

Where the same stage appears in two sources, the figures differ by a kilometre or two, and the sources do not say why. Treat each figure as a good guide, not a measurement.

## A longer way to Urdax

The French association offers a second route from Bayonne to Urdax, by Saint-Pée-sur-Nivelle and Sare. On its figures this is ~24 km to Saint-Pée and ~19 km on to Urdax, so ~43 km against ~36 km by Ainhoa. It leaves the Nive early, passes through Sare, and shares part of its path with the long-distance walking path GR 10, with red and white marks, as far as the frontier. This book follows the way by Ainhoa, which Visit Navarra and Eroski both describe.

## Making a hard day easier

You can change the day that troubles you without leaving the road.

- **Otsondo.** The directory ends its third stage at Amaiur/Maya, only ~10 km, so the climb comes early and the rest of the day is yours. Eroski says the opposite is possible: Day 3 can be lengthened to Berroeta, ~28 km, which joins Days 3 and 4 into one long day with the climb at the start.
- **Belate.** Eroski suggests going ~5 km beyond Berroeta on Day 4, to the inn on the climb, so the pass comes at the end of a shorter day. The directory points out that Lantz, ~15 km from Berroeta and ~4 km short of Olagüe, lies on the normal route and can end the day if the pass takes longer than planned. In both cases, check that there is a bed before you rely on the plan.
- **The last day.** Arre is ~20 km from Olagüe, and Pamplona is ~5 km further. You may want the old town as the end of your walk, or prefer to sleep at the junction.

## Rest days

With six days of walking you may not want a rest day at all. Two places suit one. **Elizondo** is the main town of the valley, with more services than the villages around it, and a day there makes sense if the weather turns before Belate: a day of waiting costs less than a mistake on the pass. **Pamplona** is a city that fills a day, and the chapter on arriving says why.

**Check before you go.** Stage ends on this road are small. Ask in the app or by phone about beds and the next day's weather, and keep a second plan.
