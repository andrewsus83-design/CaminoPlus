## H. Notes for the owner

- **The history of the winter use is contested between sources.** The brief said the road was "traditionally used in winter when the passes of the Francés were snowed in". The Xunta says "se piensa que pudo" (it is thought it may have), the Confraternity says there is "as yet no firm evidence", the Castilla y León guide says pilgrims used it until 1788 and Wikipedia says "traditionally". The book gives all four.
- **The date of recognition differs**: 2015 (CSJ, for the Pilgrim's Office), 2016 (Xunta press notes, Wikipedia). The book states both and does not choose.
- **The brief said "through Ourense province".** Only the Valdeorras part is in Ourense (~35 km, computed); most of the road is in León (~43 km), Lugo, Pontevedra and, at the end, A Coruña.
- **The Xunta route page says 239.2 km but its stages start at Las Médulas**, ~27 km from Ponferrada; the leaflet and the catalog say ~260 km. The Xunta page's own text says "somewhat less than 250 km" and "about 240 km".
- **Compostela**: the book gives arithmetic (Chantada ~104 km, Monforte ~135 km) with the Confraternity's different figure (~120 km at Monforte), and sends the reader to the Office. It does not say that any start counts.
