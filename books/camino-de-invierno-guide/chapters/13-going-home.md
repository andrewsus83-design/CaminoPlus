# Going home

## The first days

Coming home from a quiet road can feel louder than coming home from a busy one. The streets are fast, the phone is full, and the days no longer have the shape of a walk. That is normal. Give yourself a few days before you decide what the road meant.

## Keep a little of it

You do not have to keep all of it. A few small habits are enough.

- **Walk.** Go out for an hour a day, as you did on the road, with no purpose except the walk.
- **Write it down.** While the days are fresh, note three things from each stage: something you saw, something you ate, something you thought. In a year you will be glad of them.
- **Keep your credencial.** The stamps are a map of where you slept and who you met.
- **Thank someone.** A host, a stranger who gave directions, someone at home who waited for you.

## Walking again

Many people want another road after their first. The Winter Way meets the Camino Francés at Ponferrada, and the French Way has its own book in this series. The Camino Inglés and the Camino Primitivo have books too, and so do the roads to Finisterre and Muxía. This road will be here, as quiet as you left it, when you want to walk it again.

*Buen Camino.*
