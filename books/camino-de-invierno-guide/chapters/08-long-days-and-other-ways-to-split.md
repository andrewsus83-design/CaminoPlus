# Long days and other ways to split

## Why this chapter exists

The Xunta's nine stages are official units, not a recommended pace, and three of them are longer than ~30 km: Quiroga to Monforte (~36 km), Monforte to Chantada (~31 km) and Bendoiro to Outeiro (~34 km). The Xunta divides each stage into sections, and the sections show where a long day can be cut.

Read the splits below as measuring points, not as promises. A point on the Xunta's list may be a village, a bridge or a hamlet, and this book does not say whether you can sleep there. Ask in the app, ask locally, and confirm the night before.

## Where the long days can be cut

All the figures are sums of the Xunta's web sections, except where a line says otherwise, and so good to a kilometre or two.

- **Quiroga to Monforte (~36 km).** The sections run ~5 km to Nocedo, ~14 km to O Carballo de Lor, ~21 km to Castroncelos and ~24 km to A Pobra do Brollón. The natural cut is A Pobra: ~24 km, then ~13 km.
- **Monforte to Chantada (~31 km).** The sections reach Moreda at ~7 km, O Reguengo at ~10 km, A Barxa at ~15 km and Belesar at ~24 km. Cut at A Barxa for ~15 km and ~16 km, or at Belesar for ~24 km and ~6 km.
- **Bendoiro to Outeiro (~34 km).** Silleda is ~9 km in, which is too early to stop, and A Bandeira ~17 km in. A Bandeira splits the day into two almost equal halves.
- **Rodeiro to Bendoiro (~28 km).** Lalín lies ~22 km in, and then ~6 km remain to A Laxe, by the Castilla y León guide's measure.
- **Las Médulas to O Barco (~26 km).** Puente de Domingo Flórez is only ~9 km in, so cutting there is rarely useful.

## A twelve-day shape

Cut the two longest days and you get twelve:

1. Ponferrada to Las Médulas, ~27 km
2. Las Médulas to O Barco de Valdeorras, ~26 km
3. O Barco de Valdeorras to A Rúa, ~14 km
4. A Rúa to Quiroga, ~27 km
5. Quiroga to A Pobra do Brollón, ~24 km
6. A Pobra do Brollón to Monforte de Lemos, ~13 km
7. Monforte de Lemos to Chantada, ~31 km
8. Chantada to Rodeiro, ~26 km
9. Rodeiro to Bendoiro, ~28 km
10. Bendoiro to A Bandeira, ~17 km
11. A Bandeira to Outeiro, ~17 km
12. Outeiro to Santiago, ~17 km

That adds up to ~267 km. The CaminoPlus website lists the road as 10 to 12 stages, and this shape is the long end of that range.

## A shorter walk

If you have fewer days, the Xunta's last five stages, from Monforte to Santiago, add up to ~135 km, and its leaflet puts the same stretch at ~134 km. The last four, from Chantada, add up to ~104 km, and the leaflet gives ~103 km. Those are not figures to plan a Compostela on without asking: the chapter on arriving explains why.

## Variants on the road

The Xunta names three choices, in walking order: the detour over the Serra da Lastra on Day 2, ~14 km longer than the main line; the option by San Clodio and Ribas de Sil on Day 5; and the shorter way round Monte Faro on Day 7, which in winter avoids the snow. The days are those of the ten-day plan.

## Lengths differ between sources

For each day, the Xunta's web pages, the Xunta's leaflet and the Castilla y León guide give different figures, by a kilometre or two, and on the longest day by ~3 km. Plan for the longer figure, and carry enough water and food for it.
