# Where to start, and how many days

## You can begin almost anywhere

Nobody has to walk all 33 stages. The road is the same road from every town on it, and it can be walked in pieces over several years. This list shows how far each usual start is from Santiago, and how many of this book's days follow it. The figures are worked out from the stage figures in this book, so they are ~ guides. Other tables differ by several kilometres over such distances: Wikipedia's example itinerary puts Pamplona ~700 km from Santiago, where Spanish Wikipedia has ~712 km.

- **Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port:** ~777 km, 33 days. The whole Way, and the steepest first day.
- **Pamplona:** ~712 km, 30 days. A city start that skips the Pyrenees.
- **Burgos:** ~491 km, 21 days. The Meseta, the mountains and Galicia.
- **León:** ~312 km, 13 days. About a fortnight, with the mountains and Galicia.
- **Astorga:** ~260 km, 11 days.
- **O Cebreiro:** ~154 km, 7 days. Galicia only.
- **Sarria:** ~115 km, 5 days. By far the most common start in 2024.

Wikipedia says that a typical walk of the whole road takes at least four weeks, with a rest day or two. Pamplona, Burgos and León are the three natural places for one, and a quiet day after the Pyrenees is a kindness to your legs.

## Sample plans

These are sums, not prescriptions, worked out from the stage figures.

- **The whole road in about five weeks:** 33 walking days, rest days at Pamplona (after day 3), Burgos (after day 12) and León (after day 20), and a last day in Santiago: 37 days.
- **Burgos to Santiago:** 21 walking days, three weeks, and a rest day in León makes 22.
- **León to Santiago:** 13 walking days, so about a fortnight with one rest day.
- **Sarria to Santiago:** 5 walking days, plus a day in Santiago.

## The Compostela, in the Office's words

The Compostela is the certificate issued by the Pilgrim's Office in Santiago. The Office says it is for those who come to the tomb of the Apostle for religious and/or spiritual reasons and follow the routes of the Way of St James on foot, by bicycle or on horseback.

On foot or on horseback, the Office asks for at least the last hundred kilometres, and by bicycle the last two hundred, shown by the credencial, stamped along the route. On foot or on horseback that means at least a hundred continuous kilometres on the same route to Santiago that the Office recognises, and the final stage must include at least the last one leading into the Cathedral.

The Way may be done in stages, if they follow one another in chronological and geographical order. A pilgrim who walks only the minimum distance must get the credencial stamped at the start and the end of each stage, with the date. A pilgrim who has already walked part of a recognised route outside Spain needs no less than seventy kilometres in Spain. The Cabildo, the cathedral's governing council, reserves the right to grant the Compostela in special and exceptional cases.

## What that means on this road

The French Way is long, so most starts are far above the limit. Sarria is the usual place to begin the last stretch, and sources put it between ~108 and ~115 km from the cathedral: Wikipedia's example itinerary gives ~108 km, Spanish Wikipedia ~113 km, Wikipedia's article on Sarria ~115 km, and the Xunta de Galicia's own stages from Sarria add up to ~115 km. Every one of those figures is more than a hundred kilometres. One stage later, Portomarín is ~93 km away by the Xunta's figures, which is less. Published figures differ, and the Office decides, not this book, so if your plan is close to the limit, or unusual, ask the Pilgrim's Office before you go.

## Splitting the days

The stage towns in this book are the usual ones, not the only ones. The longest days, 7, 8, 26 and 31, are each ~28 to ~30 km, and day 17, with ~17 to ~18 km between villages, is better planned with food and water in mind. Wikipedia's example itinerary splits the road differently in several places, for example at Rabanal del Camino, ~20 km after Astorga, or at Calzadilla de la Cueza, ~17 km after Carrión. Choose the plan that suits your legs, and look at the app for where to sleep.
