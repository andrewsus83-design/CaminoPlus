## F. Advice, not facts (no source needed, kept plain)

Train on hills with the pack you will carry; test shoes on a long day; layers, hat, gloves, a dry bag for one dry layer; save maps before the mountains; carry food and water for the days that pass few villages; start early; face traffic where you must walk on a road, keep to the edge, wear something bright, never walk a road in the dark or in thick mist; stop at the first hot spot; rest a day for a pain that grows; see a doctor for anything that worries you; tell someone your plan; earplugs and a head torch in dormitories; check mattress seams and keep the pack off the bed; keep valuables close; in mist, stop if the next marker cannot be seen and go back to the last one; the decision rule for the Hospitales (choose the lower road in bad forecasts, mist, wind, a late start, tiredness or inexperience; on the high road carry food, water, warm layers and a map, tell someone, turn back if mist closes in); a rest day in Lugo, Oviedo or Santiago; ask the Office about any start other than Oviedo. Marked `advice`.
