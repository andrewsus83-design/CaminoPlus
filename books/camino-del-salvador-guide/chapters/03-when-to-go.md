# When to go

## The mountain makes its own weather

The Confraternity of St James says it plainly. In León province July and August are very hot, much as on the Camino Francés. The weather on the crossing of the Cordillera is highly variable, with thick fog, heavy rain and severe cold all possible, and the Asturian side can be very different from the León side on the same day. Spanish Wikipedia describes the Puerto de Pajares, the road pass over the range, as known for its winter snowfalls and for fog on summer nights.

The numbers below are for the two cities at the ends, and neither is the mountain. Wikipedia gives them from the Spanish state weather agency, AEMET, for 1991 to 2020. León's station stands at ~916 m, Oviedo's at ~336 m, and the walk climbs from ~837 m at León to a high point of ~1,568 m. Expect the crossing to be colder and wetter than either table.

- **León, winter.** The January average high is ~7 °C and the average low ~−1 °C. The lowest temperature on record is ~−17 °C.
- **León, summer.** The July average high is ~27 °C, and the record is ~38 °C. July is the driest month (~18 mm of rain), in a year of ~496 mm.
- **Oviedo, winter.** The January average high is ~12 °C and the low ~5 °C. The record low is ~−6 °C.
- **Oviedo, summer.** The August average high is ~24 °C, and the record ~41 °C.
- **Oviedo, rain.** ~1,028 mm a year. July is the driest month (~44 mm) and November the wettest (~134 mm).

León is high and dry, Oviedo green and wet. The walk passes from one to the other over the range, so you meet both.

## Seasons

**Winter.** The association of the León municipalities says snow often begins in December, or somewhat later, and that nobody can predict it: some winters bring hardly any, others heavy falls. Its advice is to heed what the hostel tells you, and if the hostel says conditions are bad, not to venture out, because the Way will always be there for another time.

**Spring and autumn.** The association says the streams of the third day can carry a lot of water in the thaw, and that the forest of Valgrande is muddy. It recommends mountain boots in spring, in autumn and, above all, in winter.

**Summer.** The association says that in summer, with good fitness, you may plan longer stages, and that the days can be very hot and the nights cool at altitude. The CaminoPlus route guide in the app, a hand-written overview and not live data, suggests June to September. No source read for this book names a best month, so treat that as a suggestion.

## Daylight

These are the book's own figures, calculated from a standard solar formula for León, in local clock time. They are approximate, so check them against a sun table before you plan. Mountain valleys lose the sun sooner than open ground.

- Late December: sunrise ~08:50, sunset ~17:50, ~9 hours of daylight.
- Early November: sunset ~18:10, ~10 hours.
- Late March: sunrise ~07:30, sunset ~19:35, ~12 hours.
- Late June: sunrise ~06:45, sunset ~22:05, ~15 hours.
- Late September: sunset ~20:20, ~12 hours.

In late autumn and winter, the association says, leave a margin of daylight for the unexpected.

## Crowds, and checking

No source read for this book gives a count of pilgrims on this road, and the Pilgrim's Office's statistics page could not be read. Whether a hostel is open in your month is a question for the app and for a call, not for this book.

**Check before you go.** Snow, fog, a swollen stream, a diverted path: they change from year to year and from day to day. AEMET publishes warnings and town forecasts, in English as well as Spanish. Read the forecast for the villages you will cross, remember that it understates the ridge above them, and ask the hostel the evening before.
