# The inner Way

## Many reasons, an old road

People walk to Santiago for faith, for sport, for grief, out of curiosity, or to rest from work. All of those reasons are welcome on this road, and it has always had more than one kind of traveller. The Xunta says that A Mezquita, where the road enters Galicia, was a land crossed by pilgrims, but also by reapers, muleteers, dealers and travellers who came for many different reasons. The Junta's guide says the road began as a link between two kingdoms, and that pilgrims came to use it afterwards. You will be walking in the steps of people who had every sort of errand.

The Zamora tourism board says that at Granja de Moreruela the pilgrimage to Santiago "begins, or rather continues", for most walkers. Some of you will arrive with weeks of the Vía de la Plata behind you, and some will start with a clean credencial. Both are at home here.

## What a quiet road gives you

A road with few people leaves long hours with nothing to do but walk. You may find that this is welcome, or that it is hard, or that it is both on the same day. There is no right answer. The Zamora plain gives you distance, and a horizon to rest your eyes on. The mountains give you effort and a reason to go slowly. The last days, among the oaks and small bridges of the Deza, give you, after A Laxe, the company of the Winter Way.

The Junta's guide reports an inscription in Valdemerilla, a village with twelve inhabitants. In translation it says that the village embodies calm and makes everything relative, and that few people here are a crowd. You do not need to walk to a village to try that thought. You can try it at the next bench.

## Questions for the walk

Carry one of these for an hour, and let it go if it does not help.

- What are you carrying that you could leave behind?
- When did you last go a whole morning without speaking?
- What is the first thing you notice when the road is empty?
- What would you say to the person you most wish were walking beside you?
- What does enough look like for you today: enough kilometres, enough water, enough rest?
- What are you walking towards, and what are you walking away from? Are they the same thing?

## In the evening

Write three lines, or none. What did you see? What did your body say? What do you need tomorrow? If you pray, this is a good hour for it. If you do not, this is a good hour for being still.
