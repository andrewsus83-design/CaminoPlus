# Going home

The Way does not end at the cathedral. It may end a few days later, in a kitchen, when you notice that you no longer hurry through a meal.

## The first week

Give yourself time. You may find the return harder than the road, and the reason is not hard to see: for days on end your whole job was to walk, eat, wash and sleep. Your body may want to keep walking. Let it. Take a short walk each day, even if it is only round the block, and eat something simple.

Look at your credencial. Each stamp marks a night and a door that opened. Keep it somewhere you will see it.

## Keeping the road

- **Write it down while you remember it.** Three lines a day is enough. The names of the villages go first.
- **Send a note to someone who gave you a bed.** A line of thanks costs little.
- **Walk again, on a smaller scale.** A local path, a spare Saturday and a flask of tea keep the habit alive.
- **Keep the questions.** Pick one from the chapter on the inner Way and carry it for a week at home.

## Walking again

If this was your first Camino, the others have their own books in this series. If you walked in from the Vía de la Plata, you already know what a long road does to a person. If you walked only the end, the beginning is still there, on the plain of Zamora, and you can walk it another year.

*Buen Camino*, and welcome home.
