# Long days and other ways to split

The sixteen days of this book follow the stage towns the sources use. They are not the only way to walk the road, and three of them are ~34 km or more. This chapter shows where each long day can be cut, and the one real choice of route. Before you count on a split, ask whether you can sleep there: that is a question for the app and for local information, and this book does not answer it.

## The sixteen days at a glance

1. Granja de Moreruela to Tábara, ~26 km
2. Tábara to Santa Marta de Tera, ~23 km
3. Santa Marta de Tera to Rionegro del Puente, ~27 km
4. Rionegro del Puente to San Salvador de Palazuelo, ~19 km
5. San Salvador de Palazuelo to Puebla de Sanabria, ~22 km
6. Puebla de Sanabria to Requejo, ~12 km
7. Requejo to Lubián, ~19 km
8. Lubián to A Gudiña, ~27 km
9. A Gudiña to Laza, ~35 km
10. Laza to Vilar de Barrio, ~19 km
11. Vilar de Barrio to Ourense, ~35 km
12. Ourense to Cea, ~23 km
13. Cea to Dozón, ~15 km
14. Dozón to Bendoiro, ~19 km
15. Bendoiro to Outeiro, ~34 km
16. Outeiro to Santiago, ~17 km

## The three longest days

- **A Gudiña to Laza (~35 km).** Campobecerros is ~21 km in and leaves ~15 km. The Xunta rates the first two sections High.
- **Vilar de Barrio to Ourense (~35 km).** Xunqueira de Ambía is ~14 km in and leaves ~21 km.
- **Bendoiro to Outeiro (~34 km).** Silleda is ~9 km in and A Bandeira ~17 km in.

On the Zamora days the Junta's table lists many villages between the stage towns. By that table, Faramontanos de Tábara is ~18.5 km from Granja de Moreruela, which leaves ~7.5 km to Tábara. Villar de Farfón is ~21 km beyond Santa Marta de Tera, which leaves ~6.5 km to Rionegro del Puente. Mombuey is ~8.5 km from Rionegro del Puente, on a day of ~19 km.

## Days you can join

- **Puebla de Sanabria to Lubián in one day** is ~31 km by the Junta's table, the sum of Days 6 and 7. It crosses the Padornelo pass, so choose it only in settled weather, and keep Requejo in mind as the place to stop.
- **Ourense to Dozón** is ~38 km, the sum of Days 12 and 13, and would skip the option of Oseira. You may prefer to keep the short day.

## The route choice: Laza or Verín

At A Gudiña the Xunta says the road has two itineraries. One is the Verea Sur by Laza, Vilar de Barrio and Xunqueira de Ambía, which this book follows. The other, called the Itinerario de Monterrei, goes by Verín, Xinzo de Limia and Allariz, and the two meet again at Ourense.

The Xunta's stage lengths for the Verín way are ~42 km from A Gudiña to Verín, ~35 km to Xinzo de Limia, ~21 km to Allariz and ~23 km to Ourense. That is ~120 km, against ~89 km by Laza, so ~31 km longer, and the Xunta's own totals differ by the same amount: ~253 km against ~222 km.

You trade the high country of the Verea Sur for a longer road through valleys and the plain of A Limia. The Xunta mentions the great castle of Monterrei, whose outline shows on the skyline as you near Verín, the former lake of A Limia, the Lagoa de Antela, once the largest freshwater lake in Spain and drained in the 1960s, and Allariz, which it calls one of the most beautiful and best preserved towns in Galicia. The Junta's guide lists pilgrims' hospitals in Verín and Monterrei. The Xunta also says the way from A Gudiña to Verín stays close to the N-525 and the A-52 motorway.

## Roads that join

The Xunta says that old Portuguese roads from the interior enter Ourense province along its whole southern border and join the Vía de la Plata at Verín, Laza, Xinzo and Ourense. The one from Chaves, by Feces, reaches Laza, where it joins the Verea Sur. The Confraternity of St James describes a road from Zamora by Bragança in Portugal that reaches the Camino Sanabrés at Verín. If you arrive from one of those roads, you will share the last days with this one.

## Starting later

If your time is short, the sums are these.

- From Puebla de Sanabria, ~255 km by the Junta's table.
- From Lubián, ~224 km by the Junta's table, and ~222 km by the Xunta's.
- From A Gudiña, ~196 km by the Xunta's lengths, and ~201 km by the Junta's table.
- From Ourense, ~107 km by the Xunta's lengths, and ~111 km by the Junta's table.

The chapter on arriving says what the Pilgrim's Office writes about the last hundred kilometres. Rest days are in the chapter on sleeping, eating and resting.
