# Ourense to Santiago

## The character of these days

Five days take you out of the city of the Miño and over the green hills of the Deza to the cathedral. By the Xunta's stage lengths they add up to ~107 km, and by the Junta's table, from 2011, to ~111 km. The Xunta rates every one of them Medium except the last, which it rates Low.

From A Laxe, in the parish of Bendoiro, you share the road with the Camino de Invierno. The Xunta says the Winter Way joins the Vía de la Plata at Lalín, and its stage pages put the meeting at A Laxe, ~50 km from Santiago. The last two days here are the last two days of *The Winter Way*, which tells them at more length.

### Day 12: Ourense to Cea (~23 km)

The stage starts at the old Roman bridge, and the Xunta gives two ways out of the city. One, by the Costiña de Canedo, follows the right bank of the Miño along the N-120. The other climbs by Cudeiro, a fine height with good views, along stretches of Camino Real with the old paving still in place. They meet again at As Casas Novas, beside the N-525.

Further on come Tamallancos and, at Sobreira, a detour to the castle-pazo of Vilamarín, then Ponte Sobreira, a handsome historic stone bridge over the River Barbantiño. San Cristovo de Cea, which the Xunta places about two kilometres from the junction, is famous for its wheat bread.

The Xunta rates the day Medium, and gives ~23 km. The Junta's table, which ends at San Cristovo de Cea, gives ~22 km.

### Day 13: Cea to Dozón (~15 km)

The shortest day of the road, and a chance to choose. From the sanctuary of A Saleta the Xunta gives two options. The shorter goes by Piñor, through oak woods and lanes between oaks called *corredoiras*, to O Castro de Dozón, a small town that is the head of its municipality. The other goes by the monastery of Oseira, a Cistercian house of the twelfth century, whose community life, the Xunta says, began in 1137 and which has a long tradition of welcoming pilgrims. The Junta places Oseira ~9 km from Cea.

The Xunta describes the way to Oseira as long stretches of woodland that can be difficult at times, in complete solitude. From the monastery you come back to Dozón, and at A Gouxa, in the municipality of Dozón, you are in the province of Pontevedra. The Xunta lists ~15 km for both options, but the way by Oseira looks longer, since the Junta puts the monastery ~9 km from Cea and the road then returns to Dozón. Ask the length locally.

### Day 14: Dozón to Bendoiro (~19 km)

You leave Dozón by the church for the Alto de Santo Domingo, ~736 m, which the Xunta calls cold country with frequent snow in the hardest months. The land is high, lonely and bare, with place names that recall a pre-Roman past, and then it drops fast to the River Dezón at Pontenoufe, ~8 km on, where you enter the municipality of Lalín.

You go on through small villages along a Camino Real that once had inns and still keeps something of their air. The Xunta names San Xoán de Botos as the home of the painter Laxeiro, and Donsión, whose Baroque church is a fine one. Lalín, capital of the Deza, lies ~4 km from Donsión. The day ends at Bendoiro, where A Laxe is the meeting point with the Winter Way. The Xunta gives ~19 km.

### Day 15: Bendoiro to Outeiro (~34 km)

The longest day of this end of the road. The first metres follow the N-525, and then the way keeps to the Deza country by Vilasoa, Prado and the medieval bridge of Taboada over the River Deza, to Silleda, ~9 km in. Beyond lie O Foxo, Chapa and A Bandeira, ~17 km in, and the *pazo* of Santo Estevo de Oca, which the Xunta says is called the Galician Versailles for its gardens.

At A Ponte Ulla you cross the River Ulla by a historic bridge and enter the municipality of Vedra, and with it the province of A Coruña. The stage ends in Vedra, at Outeiro. The Xunta rates the day Medium and gives ~34 km. Silleda and A Bandeira are natural places to split it.

### Day 16: Outeiro to Santiago (~17 km)

After Rubial a detour leads to the Pico Sacro, the Xunta says more than five hundred metres high, tied to the tradition of the bulls that the Apostle's disciples tamed to carry his body to its tomb. Then the way passes A Susana and enters Santiago by the Sar district. After the bridge over the Sar there is one last effort: a hard climb up the Rúa do Sar and the Rúa do Castrón Douro. At the top you pass under the Porta de Mazarelos, the only gate that still stands of the old medieval wall, and the last steps go by the Praza da Universidade, the Rúa da Calderería and the Praza das Praterías to the Praza do Obradoiro.

The Xunta rates the day Low and gives ~17 km. The Junta's table, which starts from Ponte Ulla, gives ~20 km.
