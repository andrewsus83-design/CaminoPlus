# Going home

The first days back are strange. The road has taught your body a rhythm of early light, one thing at a time and a short list of needs, and home does not run on it. Expect to miss the walking before you miss anything else.

Keep what you can. Walk to the shop and not to the car. Eat one meal a day without a screen, as the road suggested. Write down three things you want to remember before they blur: a bridge, a face, a hill. Put the credencial somewhere you will see it. A stamp is a small thing, and a full card of them is a map of a fortnight you will not live again in quite that way.

Tell the story, and listen when someone else tells theirs. Every pilgrim has one, and every one is different.

If you want to walk again, the road has neighbours. The Coastal Way, in this series, goes from Porto to Santiago beside the sea. The walk from Santiago to Finisterre and Muxía, ~90 km in the catalog, goes on to the ocean. The French Way, ~773 km, is a longer commitment. The CaminoPlus app lists them all.

Whatever you do next, thank the people who kept the road open for you: the friends of the Camino, the hosts and the people in the villages. *Bom caminho*, and *Buen Camino*, again.
