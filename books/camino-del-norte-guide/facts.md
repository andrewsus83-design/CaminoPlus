## H. Advice, not facts (no source needed, kept plain)

Train with the pack you will carry; start at an easy pace; treat a hot spot at once; change socks at lunch; carry a dry layer in a dry bag; face the traffic where you must walk on a road and do not walk it in poor light; stay back from cliff edges when the sea is rough; tell someone your plan; use earplugs; keep your pack off the floor and tell the host about bedbugs; plan rest days; write down what you remember; the questions in "The inner Way".
