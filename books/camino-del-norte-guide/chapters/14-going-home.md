# Going home

The first days at home can feel odd. The early alarm may now wake nobody. The pack that held everything you needed sits empty on the floor, and the list of things you thought you needed may be shorter than it was. This is ordinary, and it passes.

## Keep what you can

- **Walk.** A short walk each day, even in the rain, keeps the rhythm of the road and keeps your legs in shape.
- **Write it down.** Write what you remember while it is fresh: the people, the places, a smell, a hill. Your credencial, with its stamps, is a record too. Keep it somewhere you will see it.
- **Keep one small habit.** The morning start, the evening wash of the socks, the silence before breakfast. Choose one to carry into ordinary days.
- **Tell your story, and listen to others'.** Some people will not understand, and that is fine. Others will have walked.
- **Say thank you.** To the hosts, to the people who kept your house, to the ones who walked with you.

## Walking again

Some people want to walk again. The Camino Primitivo, which the Norte can join, and the road to the end of the earth at Finisterre and Muxía each have a book in this series, and the app lists other routes. There is no hurry. A Camino can wait, and so can you.

*Buen Camino*, today and the day after.
